Background & Photographic Philosophy

Photography is more than clicking a shutter button—it is the art of capturing authentic emotions, shaping available and artificial light, and crafting timeless narratives. Through internship and professional production at Cortez Photography, this portfolio encompasses hundreds of curated sessions across diverse genres.

Every shoot is prepared meticulously, from initial visual concept moodboards and optimal lens focal length selection to precision color retouching in Adobe Lightroom, creating warm, elegant, and cohesive visual aesthetics.

Photography Production Workflow

A systematic workflow from initial planning to master delivery:

01

Pre-Production & Moodboard Planning

Developing creative concepts, scouting locations, planning wardrobe, creating pose guides, and timing natural light.

02

On-Location / Studio Shooting

Executing photoshoots with professional mirrorless/DSLR gear, fine-tuned manual exposures, and light modifiers (softboxes/reflectors).

03

Photo Curation & Color Grading

Filtering top RAW captures, exposure balancing, white balance calibration, skin tone retouching, and applying signature color profiles.

04

High-Resolution Export & Delivery

Exporting uncompressed high-resolution files for print reproduction alongside web-optimized versions for social media platforms.

Technical Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Managing high dynamic range conditions (intense outdoor contrast or extreme low-light evening reception venues).

Solution: Captured uncompressed 14-bit RAW files to leverage maximum dynamic range, deployed high-diffusion off-camera flashes, and performed selective post-processing masking to restore highlight and shadow fidelity.
